DocuMind

Ask questions about your own documents and get streamed answers that cite their sources.

Benchmarked on questions phrased in the document's words and on paraphrases that share none of them.

Try the live demoCode on GitHub

The problem

Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) answers questions from your documents, but only as well as it retrieves the right passage. Keyword search misses paraphrases; embedding search misses exact codes and numbers.

How it works

  • Hybrid retrieval: BM25 keyword search, implemented from scratch, fused with Gemini embeddings using Reciprocal Rank Fusion.
  • Answers stream token by token with inline citations; a second model call checks how well each answer is supported by its sources.
  • A benchmark of 40 labelled questions, half paraphrased, plus questions with no answer, to measure retrieval and refusals.
  • Per-visitor rate limits and a daily budget so a public demo can't drain the API quota.

What I learned

Measure before you claim

The keyword-only benchmark shows exactly why hybrid search is needed: near-perfect on the document's wording, and lost on paraphrases.

Limitations

  • The benchmark is small (16 chunks), and the questions were written knowing the documents.
  • Faithfulness is judged by a language model: an indicator, not ground truth.
